The ISO 17636-1 standard specifies radiographic testing of welded joints and is
referenced alongside ASME Section XI in Acme Power's inspection procedures.
Dr. Elena Marsh presented the updated procedures at the 2025 Nuclear Materials
Reliability Conference held in Houston.

The conference, organized by EPRI, brought together regulators from the Nuclear
Regulatory Commission, vendors such as Westinghouse and Framatome, and operators
including Acme Power. A key theme was the management of stress-corrosion cracking
across the operating fleet.

Following the conference, Acme Power committed to a fleet-wide rollout of
phased-array ultrasonic testing by 2027, coordinated by Dr. Marsh and supported
by EPRI's materials reliability program.
