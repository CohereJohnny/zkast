Acme Power operates the Riverbend Nuclear Station, a Generation III+ pressurized
water reactor located in the Gulf Coast region. The station's chief engineer,
Dr. Elena Marsh, leads the aging-management program for the reactor pressure
vessel and primary coolant piping.

In 2024, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued an inspection finding that
required Acme Power to expand its nondestructive examination (NDE) coverage of
welds in the reactor coolant system. The finding referenced ASME Boiler and
Pressure Vessel Code Section XI, which governs in-service inspection of nuclear
power plant components.

Dr. Marsh's team adopted phased-array ultrasonic testing, a process that
improves detection of stress-corrosion cracking in Inconel 600 nozzles. The
upgraded inspection program mitigates the risk of primary water stress-corrosion
cracking, a degradation mechanism that affects nickel-based alloys.
