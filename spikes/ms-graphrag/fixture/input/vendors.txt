Westinghouse supplies the reactor coolant pumps used at Riverbend Nuclear
Station, while Framatome provides the steam generator replacement components.
The Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI) published report EPRI 1012081,
which documents materials-degradation data for austenitic stainless steel
piping in pressurized water reactors.

Acme Power's procurement lead, Tomas Vega, manages the supplier qualification
process for safety-related equipment. Tomas works for Acme Power and coordinates
with both Westinghouse and Framatome on the Riverbend modernization project.

The modernization project, launched in early 2025, integrates digital
instrumentation and control systems. Bluewave Controls, a vendor based in the
Pacific Northwest, delivers the new control room equipment and integrates it
with the existing plant process computer.
